# water-kg
All materials for constructing the knowledge representing water (surface and subsurface) features; i.e., anything to do with water and water networks (hydrology).

## Groundwater - Aquifers, wells, springs, etc.
These generally come from individual states, though there are national aquifer maps and efforts to create nationwide water well databases.

## Surface water - Waterbodies, like lakes, rivers, and wetlands
This information comes primarily from USGS NHDPlus data (NHDWaterbody and NHDFlowline).

### Flowlines
How surface waterbodies connect so water can flow. This is modeled from USGS NHDPlus data (NHDFlowline).

### Catchments
Flowlines are one representation of catchments - they have a single inflow and a single outflow point.

### Watersheds
Broader than catchments, watersheds may have multiple inflow points. They are most similar to dendritic catchments. Data comes from the Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD).

## Drinking Water
Drinking water is generally sourced from aquifers via wells and/or from surface water bodies.

### Public Water Systems
The Safe Drinking Water Information System (SDWIS) provides data on public water systems, community water systems, and both transient and non-transient non-community water systems.

### Source Water Protection Areas
These are areas around water intake points (groundwater or surface water) from which water may seep into aquifers or flow into surface water bodies.
