## flowlines

Flowlines are atomic segments of streams, rivers, canals, etc. SAWGraph uses flowlines from the NHDPlusV2 dataset to navigate surface water networks both upstream and downstream.

## groundwater

Groundwater refers to aquifers of various kinds, largely divided into sand/gravel/alluvial and bedrock. Data comes from both the USGS as well as individual states.

## sdwis

Data for public water systems from the Safe Drinking Water Information System.

## surface_water

Here we include lakes, ponds, wetlands, etc. SAWGraph uses water bodies from the NHDPlusV2.

## wbd

The Watershed Boundary Dataset includes watersheds at different levels of resolution, from regional (HUC2) to local (HUC12). The Hydrologic Unit Code (HUC) system is a also used within the NHDPlusV2 dataset.

## wells

Water wells (both public and private) are a primary source of water for roughly half of the people in the United States. Private water well data is generally stored at the state level, though there are efforts to create a national database.
